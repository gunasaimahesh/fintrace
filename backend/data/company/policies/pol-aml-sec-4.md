4.2 Waiving an onboarding control for a merchant that processes more than $250,000 per month
requires approval from an officer with L3 clearance.

4.5 Freezing a live merchant or a linked ring of accounts requires L3 approval, and infrastructure
shared with unrelated customers must be excluded from the freeze.
